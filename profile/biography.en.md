# Ibragim Valikhodjaev — Biography, Exhibitions and Artist CV

[https://ibragim-valikhodjaev.blogspot.com/2026/10/blog-post.html](<https://ibragim-valikhodjaev.blogspot.com/2026/10/blog-post.html>)

**Ibragim Valikhodjaev** (Ибрагим Валиходжаев) is a professional Uzbek painter and sculptor, art expert, curator and organiser of international art projects based in Uzbekistan, Tashkent. He is Chairman of the Union of Artists of Uzbekistan and an Academician of the Academy of Arts of Uzbekistan.

Born in 1958, he explores contemporary life, memory and Uzbek culture through a wide range of genres and series: Namangan bazaar scenes and everyday life; RED; early monochrome and restrained-palette paintings; nude figure studies, portraits and still lifes; village landscapes and plein air paintings of Uzbekistan, including Miraki; Montenegro landscapes; monumental abstraction; and metal sculpture, including floating works. His continuing research into colour, form, rhythm and composition connects these different directions.

He combines studio practice with the organisation and curation of exhibitions and international sculpture symposia. He has organised and curated eight symposia in Turkey and Uzbekistan and participated in plein air projects in both countries. His international work and creative visits also include China, Azerbaijan, Cyprus, Georgia and Kazakhstan.

As an art expert, he founded and chairs the Expert Commission for the Evaluation of Artworks in Uzbekistan and has developed valuation rates for artistic works.

*Alternative transliterations of his name include Ibrahim Valikhodjaev and Ibrokhim Valikhodjaev.*

## Institutional roles and teaching

- **2026–present:** Lecturer in the master's programme, Kimyo International University in Tashkent.
- **2023–present:** Academician of the Academy of Arts of Uzbekistan.
- **2017–present:** Chairman of the Union of Artists of Uzbekistan.
- **2017–present:** Founder and Chairman of the Expert Commission for the Evaluation of Artworks in the Republic of Uzbekistan; developed valuation rates for artistic works.
- **Until 2026:** Head of Artistic Works and member of the Expert Council during the construction of the Center of Islamic Civilization in Uzbekistan, Tashkent.
- **1999–2012:** Chairman of the Art Fund of the Republic of Uzbekistan.
- **1997–1999:** Chairman, Namangan branch of the Union of Artists of Uzbekistan.
- **1997–1999:** Chairman, Namangan branch of the Art Fund of Uzbekistan.

## Education and gallery founding

**1986–1991:** Studio of Master Rahim Ahmedov; Faculty of Painting, Alexander Nikolayevich Ostrovsky Institute. See the master's painting [«For Whom» at Sotheby's](<https://www.sothebys.com/en/auctions/ecatalogue/2014/at-the-crossroads-2-l14009/lot.13.html>).

**2021–2026:** Founder, Valikhodjaev Gallery House, Uzbekistan, Tashkent; opened 5 August 2021.

**1993–1999:** Founder, «Tumor» Gallery Salon, Uzbekistan, Namangan; the first private art gallery in Uzbekistan.

## Public commissions and monumental art

His work includes the organisation and leadership of artistic works for government buildings, cultural institutions, transport infrastructure and public monuments.

- **Senate of Uzbekistan (Upper House of Parliament), Uzbekistan, Tashkent:** Organised and led the artistic works for the Senate building.
- **State Conservatory of Uzbekistan, Uzbekistan, Tashkent:** Organised and led the artistic works for the conservatory building.
- **Center of Islamic Civilization, Uzbekistan, Tashkent:** Head of Artistic Works throughout the construction of the Center; directed and organised the artistic works and served on its Expert Council. Work continued until 2026.
- **Tashkent International Airport, Terminal 2, Uzbekistan, Tashkent:** Organised and led the artistic works for the international airport terminal.
- **«Friendship of Peoples» metro station, Uzbekistan, Tashkent:** Organised and led artistic works for the metro station.
- **Memorial at the mass graves and other public monuments, Uzbekistan:** Artistic work on the memorial at the mass graves and other monuments in public spaces.
- **Other state and cultural commissions, Uzbekistan:** Artistic direction for government premises, parks, palaces of culture and large public complexes; organisation and coordination of artistic works on state projects.

## Additional teaching and institutional experience

Archival records also document his work as a tutor at the Mannon Uygur Tashkent State Art Institute (1991–1994), Chairman of the Special Fund of the Academy of Arts of Uzbekistan (1999–2005), teacher at the Kamoliddin Bekhzod National Institute of Arts and Design (2006–2007), and Head of Creative Activities and Exhibition Coordination at the Academy of Arts of Uzbekistan (2017–2018). The Special Fund is listed separately from his chairmanship of the Art Fund of the Republic of Uzbekistan (1999–2012).

His 50th-birthday exhibition at the Central Exhibition Hall in Uzbekistan, Tashkent, in 2008 presented more than 200 works. That year he was also awarded the Gold Medal of the Academy of Arts of Uzbekistan. The updated CV includes further exhibitions documented in the archive, including international exhibitions in the United States in 1996.

## Passing on experience: a masterclass in Xi’an

In September 2025, Valikhodjaev led a painting masterclass for students at Xi’an Academy of Fine Arts in China. He took part in the 14–19 September visit associated with CASIE’s ‘One Belt, One Road — Central Asia’ programme as an artist and chairman of the Union of Artists of Uzbekistan. The composition, completed in one hour and twenty minutes, was donated to the Academy. The masterclass video below follows the development of the image, from its first lines to the relationships between areas of colour and the finished composition.
